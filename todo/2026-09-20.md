# 2026-09-20

## What We Fixed

- The AI Patterns talk no longer presents four equal topics or sends attendees through a grid of unavailable source links.
- AI Patterns now appears in the public presentation gallery; the live route was deployed but its registry entry was still marked as a draft.
- Openstage no longer labels a generic cyan-purple palette as Human Quest. Its default theme now uses the deep red, coral, orange and gold carried by the Human Quest mark, with warm dark surfaces that match the identity.

## What We Built

- The talk now follows three live dives: Feedback with a brief Openstage proof, Walkthroughs, and Console + MCP.
- The closing sends everyone to the single AI Patterns take-home page used by the event QR.
- Rebuilt the visual argument around three full-screen, scroll-bound chapter stages: amber signal capture, cyan walkthrough evidence, and violet controlled agency.
- Added real product proof to the Walkthrough section, a live feedback evidence surface, and a two-sided Console/MCP control room instead of repeating bordered card grids.
- Added a reusable `VisualStage` primitive and compact-spacing controls for `StickyMedia`, raising the visual ceiling for future Openstage talks.
- Rebuilt the closing as a focused final frame with a single scannable QR above its destination, replacing the oversized gray copy and disconnected link.

## Key Decisions

- Openstage supports the Feedback story instead of taking a full demo slot.
- Human Quest is the default Openstage preset. Spectrum remains available as an optional generic theme, and browsers that saved the old mislabeled preset migrate to the corrected Human Quest theme.
- Each major system now has its own semantic color and visual grammar so the audience can recognize the transition before reading the heading.
- Hangar shows the human operator's view; MCP shows the controlled actions available to an agent.
- DoneOS MCP and Inner Quest MCP are presented as features of their products,
  with direct links into the corresponding Walkthrough Studio evidence rather
  than as standalone projects.
