# 2026-09-28 — Phase 4: the deck is the stage

## What We Built

**A deck can now have as many 3D moments as its story needs.** A new `<Stage>`
puts one WebGL canvas behind the whole deck, and sections open `<StageView>`
windows into it. Before, every 3D section opened its own canvas: each cost a
WebGL context (browsers stop at about sixteen), kept rendering after it
scrolled away, and printed as a grey box — which is why the 3D primitives
shipped in Phase 1 appeared in one catalog deck and nowhere else.

**ParticleMorph — the new signature moment.** Up to 42,000 GPU particles flow
between words, images and ten procedural shapes (sphere, globe, torus, helix,
galaxy, wave, cube, snowflake, ring, scatter). Each particle leaves on its own
schedule and rides a noise field in transit, so a change reads as a swarm
re-forming rather than a crossfade. Scroll- or step-driven; the cursor parts
the cloud. Text is drawn by the browser before it is sampled, so every script
works: Arabic joins correctly, which matters for the Doha decks.

**NeuralField.** A procedural neural network — no model file — with pulses on
every connection. Scroll can drive how much of it fires and whether it fires
at random or rolls forward layer by layer.

**Stagecraft (`/stagecraft`)** shows all of it, in the order a deck author
meets it, including a live readout of the tier the viewer's device got.

## What We Fixed

- `ScrollCamera3D` moved at a speed tied to the display's refresh rate (twice
  as fast on 120 Hz screens); it now damps by elapsed time. It also stops
  rendering off screen, and prints its `poster` instead of a grey placeholder.
- Glow on a transparent canvas: `THREE.AdditiveBlending` squares alpha, so the
  page composited every particle far dimmer than it was shaded. The stage uses
  its own blend that accumulates alpha honestly.

## Key Decisions

- **Quality is detected locally**, from the WebGL renderer string — no
  benchmark download, so a venue laptop with no internet still gets the right
  budget. `?stage=low|mid|high|off` lets a presenter override on the night.
- **Posters are the print story.** A view renders its `poster` when printing or
  on a device without WebGL; the deck reads as a considered document.
- **Views draw behind their section's content.** The Stage paints the deck
  background, then the canvas, then the content, so vignettes and text layer
  over 3D without z-index fights. Sections hosting a view are transparent.
- **No new dependencies.** three, React Three Fiber, drei and maath were
  already installed; Phase 4 is about using them.

## Verified

- Headless Chromium on the real GPU (Apple M5 Max): hero morph through five
  targets, three concurrent views in one context, Arabic morph, neural field
  at idle and forward pass, device readout.
- `?print`: no canvas created; posters render; runways collapse to one page.
- A hidden browser pane pauses rendering — an empty-stage screenshot from a
  hidden pane is not evidence. Recorded in CLAUDE.md.
